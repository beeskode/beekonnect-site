Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026 · BeeKonnect+ 1.0 for iPhone, Android phones and Wear OS watches.

BeeKonnect+ has no servers and collects nothing. Data moves only between your own iPhone and your own watch or Android phone, directly over Bluetooth. No analytics, no crash reporting service, no ads, no accounts.

Permissions and why

iPhone: Bluetooth (connect to your devices), notifications (Ring iPhone, replies), calendar, location while in use (weather), contacts (caller photos, reply recipients), Health, write only (save your watch's data), reminders, Focus status. Each is asked for when you turn the matching feature on.

Android: Bluetooth connect/advertise (the link), notifications (show your iPhone's), full-screen intent (incoming calls over the lock screen), foreground service (keep the link up), boot (reconnect after a restart), activity recognition and heart rate (health, workouts), location (workout routes, only while a workout is recording), phone state (watch only, with Talk on the watch: whether the watch's own Phone app has a call, so BeeKonnect+ doesn't ring too; nothing is read or stored), Health Connect read access to sleep, weight and oxygen saturation, with background reading (phone only; steps and heart rate aren't read, so they're never counted twice).

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Turn a feature off with its switch under Share with your devices in the iPhone app; the devices stop receiving that data. Permissions can also be withdrawn at any time in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security) or Android Settings → Apps → BeeKonnect+ → Permissions.
  • Apple Health: what BeeKonnect+ saved is yours and stays in the Health app. Delete it there (Health app → your profile picture → Apps → BeeKonnect+ → Delete All Data), and stop new writes in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → BeeKonnect+.
  • Health Connect: revoke BeeKonnect+'s access in Health Connect's app permissions.
  • Everything else lives only on your devices: Forget a device in the iPhone app, clear the app's storage on Android, or uninstall the apps to remove it all. There is nothing on any server for us to delete, because we never receive your data.

Children

BeeKonnect+ isn't directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes
