BeeKonnect+ Support

Use your Wear OS watch or Android phone with your iPhone: notifications, calls, music, calendar, weather, reminders and Apple Health, directly over Bluetooth. No account, no cloud.

iPhone in your pocket, but the watch you love runs Wear OS? Or an Android phone as your second phone? BeeKonnect+ is for you. I built it for my own setup: an iPhone for everything, an Android phone on the side, and an Android watch I'd rather wear. They didn't work together, so I made them.

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 18 or later and the BeeKonnect+ app from the App Store.
  • A Wear OS 5+ watch or an Android 13+ phone with BeeKonnect+ from Google Play.
  • A Wear OS watch is set up once with an Android phone (any phone, even a borrowed one) before it can connect to your iPhone. After that, it doesn't need that phone.

Getting started

  1. On the watch or Android phone, open BeeKonnect+ and tap Start. Follow its setup steps.
  2. On the iPhone, open BeeKonnect+ and tap Add device, then choose your device.
  3. Accept the Bluetooth pairing request on the iPhone and allow the device to receive your notifications.
  4. In the iPhone app, tap Send test notification: it should appear on your device within a few seconds.

Common questions

Notifications don't appear on my device

On the iPhone, open Settings → Bluetooth, tap ⓘ next to your device and turn on Share System Notifications. On the device, make sure BeeKonnect+ may post notifications and isn't restricted by battery saving (the app's Setup checklist links to each setting).

My device disconnects after I swipe the iPhone app away

iOS stops Bluetooth apps you swipe away in the app switcher. Open BeeKonnect+ again and your devices reconnect. There's no need to swipe it away: it uses very little battery in the background.

Can I reply to messages or call back from the watch?

Yes. Pick a reply or dictate one; your iPhone shows a notification, and one tap there opens the messaging app with the text filled in, or starts the call. iOS doesn't let any app send messages or place calls on its own.

Can I talk on calls through my watch?

On a Galaxy Watch, yes. In BeeKonnect+ on the watch, turn on Talk on the watch under Settings. Then on the iPhone open Settings → Bluetooth and tap your watch so it says Connected. iPhone calls then ring in the watch's Phone app, with sound on the watch. The watch takes calls from one phone at a time. Android phones can answer and decline, but the sound stays on the iPhone.

Can I set up my Wear OS watch with just an iPhone?

Not yet. Wear OS watches finish their first setup only with an Android phone. Set the watch up once with any Android phone, install BeeKonnect+ on the watch from Google Play, and then add it in the iPhone app.

What does Full Unlock include?

Notifications, calls, music, battery and Ring iPhone are free. Full Unlock is a one-time purchase in the iPhone app, with no subscription. It adds calendar, weather, reminders, Focus, contact photos, replies and call-backs, live workouts and Apple Health. To get it back on a new iPhone, tap Restore Purchases in About. The Android apps are free.

What does Ring iPhone do?

It plays a sound on your iPhone for up to 30 seconds, even in silent mode, so you can find it nearby. Stop it from your device, from the notification, or by opening the app.

How do I stop sharing something?

Turn off its switch under Share with your devices in the iPhone app. See the privacy policy for how to delete data.
